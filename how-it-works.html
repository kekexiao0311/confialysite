The purchase flow

From a Quote to an Active Policy, in Six Clear Steps

The same flow applies to all 15 product lines. Nothing here promises instant issuance or names a payment provider — those details depend on the insurer and plan you choose, and are shown before you buy.

Step

1. Quote form

Plain questions, one per screen. Progress saves automatically as a draft you can resume later.

Step

2. Compare quotes

Real offers from multiple insurers — price, coverage highlights, insurer name — side by side.

Step

3. Choose your plan

Coverage highlights and the standard disclaimer are restated before you move to payment.

Step

4. Pay securely

Enter your payment details to complete your purchase.

Step

5. Policy confirmation

We confirm as soon as it's ready and email you — check status anytime in your dashboard.

Step

6. Your dashboard

Drafts you can resume, and active policies with documents, in one place.
