What's covered

What Does Accident Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Lump-sum injury payout. A one-off payment for qualifying injuries, sized by severity on most plans.
  • Medical expense reimbursement. Out-of-pocket costs for accident-related treatment, useful alongside — or instead of — a full health plan.
  • Temporary disability income. A regular payment while you're unable to work, up to a set number of weeks or months.
  • Permanent disability payout. A larger lump sum if an accident results in permanent incapacity.
  • Scarring & disfigurement benefit. An extra payout for permanent scarring, calculated against a fixed injury scale (baremo de lesiones).
  • Dental injury cover. A separate benefit for teeth broken or lost in a covered accident, distinct from routine dental insurance.
  • Orthopedic aids & rehabilitation. Crutches, wheelchairs and physiotherapy needed while you recover from a covered accident.
  • Accidental death benefit. A payout to beneficiaries if the accident is fatal, usually separate from a life policy.
  • 24/7 or work-hours-only cover. Some plans only cover accidents on the job; others cover any time — confirm which applies before assuming you're covered off-hours.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether cover is 24/7 or work-hours-only — this materially changes what “protected” actually means for you, especially if your risk comes from a hobby rather than your job.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Accident Insurance in Spain

How is accident insurance different from health insurance?

Health insurance covers medical treatment; accident insurance can also cover lost income or pay a lump sum after a qualifying injury — the two solve different problems.

Does accident insurance cover accidents at work only, or anytime?

Depends on the plan — some cover 24/7, others work-related only. This is shown clearly so you know exactly what you're buying.

Is this worth it if I already have health insurance?

For many autónomos and freelancers, yes — the two typically serve different purposes and are often held together for full protection.
