What's covered

What Does Professional Liability Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Professional errors & negligence. Claims that your advice, work or deliverable caused the client a loss.
  • Legal defense costs. Legal representation if a client pursues a claim, even one that's later dismissed.
  • Defense costs outside the indemnity limit. On better plans, legal defense spend doesn't eat into the amount left over to compensate the client.
  • Client financial loss compensation. Compensation if a covered error genuinely cost the client money, up to the policy limit.
  • Loss of client documents. The cost of reconstructing files or records lost or damaged while in your care.
  • Unintentional IP infringement. Claims of accidental copyright or trademark infringement in your work product.
  • Breach of professional duty. Claims related to confidentiality or duty-of-care breaches specific to regulated professions.
  • Extended reporting period. Claims made about past work for a set period after you stop trading — useful if you retire or close the business.
  • Retroactive cover. Some plans cover claims about past work even after the policy started — confirm the retroactive date, since it's easy to assume incorrectly that all past work is covered.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether your specific profession or service type is explicitly included — generic plans sometimes exclude niche professions, so confirm your exact activity is named, not just “consulting” in general.
