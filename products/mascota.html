What's covered

What Does Pet Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Vet consultations & treatment. Standard consultations, prescriptions and outpatient treatment.
  • Surgery. Emergency and planned surgical procedures, usually the largest single cost a policy protects against.
  • Hospitalization. Overnight stays and intensive care after surgery or a serious illness, separate from same-day treatment.
  • Diagnostic tests & imaging. Bloodwork, X-rays and scans needed to reach a diagnosis, usually bundled with treatment cover.
  • Medication. Prescribed medication linked to a covered treatment or condition.
  • Preventive care add-on. Annual vaccinations, deworming and check-ups — usually a separate wellness add-on, not standard cover.
  • Death or euthanasia cover. A payout or cremation cost if a covered illness or accident results in your pet's death.
  • Third-party liability. Covers damage or injury your pet causes to another person, animal or property.
  • Legally required liability (PPP breeds). Certain breeds classified as Potentially Dangerous Dogs have a specific legal liability requirement, separate from general pet cover.

Worth checking before you buy

Annual coverage caps, and whether your pet's breed has any exclusions or higher pricing — ask for the cap in euros, not just “up to the plan limit.”

Exact coverage, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and plan you select — full policy conditions are shown before you buy.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Pet Insurance in Spain

Are older pets still eligible for insurance in Spain?

Often yes, though price and coverage can vary by age — this is factored transparently into your quote, not hidden until a claim.

Does pet insurance cover routine vaccinations or just emergencies?

Some plans include a wellness allowance for routine care, others are emergency-only — we show you both types so you can compare true value.

Is liability included if my dog is a breed with legal requirements in Spain?

Certain breeds (Potentially Dangerous Dogs / PPP) have specific legal liability requirements — we flag this clearly during your quote so you're not caught out.
