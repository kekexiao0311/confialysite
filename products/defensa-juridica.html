What's covered

What Does Legal Defense Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Legal advice & consultation. Access to a lawyer for questions and guidance, often the most-used part of the policy.
  • 24/7 legal helpline. Phone access to a lawyer outside office hours, useful for an urgent question that can't wait for an appointment.
  • Contract drafting & review. Having a lawyer draft or check over a contract or legal document before you sign it.
  • Legal representation costs. Lawyer and procedural costs if a covered dispute goes to a formal claim or court.
  • Employment disputes. Disputes with an employer over dismissal, wages or contract terms, on many plans.
  • Consumer disputes. Disputes with a business over a faulty product or service.
  • Property/tenancy disputes. Disputes with a landlord or neighbor over a tenancy or property issue.
  • Traffic & driving disputes. Contesting a fine or defending your license points, separate from what your car insurance covers.
  • Inheritance & family disputes. Advice and representation for succession, divorce or other family conflicts.

Worth checking before you buy

The maximum payout per case, and whether disputes already underway are excluded — most policies only cover disputes that start after you buy, not ones already in progress.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Legal Defense Insurance in Spain

What is legal defense insurance, exactly?

It covers legal advice and representation costs if you're involved in a covered dispute — for example, with an employer, a landlord, or a business you've hired.

Does legal defense insurance cover criminal cases?

Typically no — this product usually covers civil, employment, or consumer disputes; check the specific plan's scope before assuming it covers everything.

Can I buy this even if I'm not currently in a dispute?

Yes — and you should. Like most insurance, it's designed to be in place before you need it, not after.
