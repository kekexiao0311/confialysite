What's covered

What Does Cyber Risk Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Data breach response. Investigation costs and required customer notifications after a breach.
  • Data recovery costs. Fees to restore or recover data that's lost, corrupted or destroyed, including specialist recovery labs when needed.
  • IT forensics & system restoration. Investigating how the breach happened and rebuilding the systems it affected.
  • Ransomware & cyber-extortion response. Costs related to a ransomware incident, on many plans.
  • Phishing & social engineering fraud. Money transferred because of a convincing fake email or call — a different risk from a technical hack, and worth confirming it's included.
  • Regulatory fines defense (GDPR/AEPD). Legal defense costs tied to a data protection investigation, and in some cases the fines themselves.
  • Third-party liability. Damages owed to a client or partner if a cyber incident on your side affected them.
  • Business interruption. Lost income if a cyber incident forces systems offline, on some plans.
  • AI-related liability. Liability tied to using AI tools that process client data, explicitly addressed on newer plans — confirm this is named, not assumed under general wording.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether your specific software/systems are covered, and whether AI-tool-related liability is explicitly included or excluded — don't assume silence means it's covered.
