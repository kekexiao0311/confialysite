What's covered

What Does Home Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Building/structure (owners). Walls, roof, fixed installations — usually required by mortgage lenders.
  • Contents. Furniture, appliances and belongings against fire, water damage and theft.
  • Water damage. One of the most common home claims in Spain — burst pipes, leaks from neighbors.
  • Fire & electrical damage. Fire, explosion and power surges that damage wiring, appliances or the structure itself.
  • Theft & break-in. Contents and sometimes cash/jewelry up to a policy limit.
  • Glass breakage. Windows, glass doors and shower screens — often excluded from bare-bones contents-only plans.
  • 24/7 emergency assistance. A locksmith, plumber, electrician or glazier dispatched any time, day or night.
  • Temporary accommodation. Hotel costs if a covered claim leaves your home unliveable while repairs are done.
  • Civil liability at home. Covers accidental injury or damage you cause to others — often bundled in as standard.

Worth checking before you buy

Frequently Asked Questions About Home Insurance in Spain

Do landlords in Spain require tenants to have home insurance?

Many do, especially for contents and liability — check your lease, and we'll help you find a plan that satisfies it exactly.

Is home insurance mandatory for owners with a mortgage?

Most mortgage lenders require at least building insurance — we flag this clearly during your quote so there's no surprise at signing.

Does home insurance cover theft in Spain?

Typically yes, up to a policy limit — full terms are shown before you buy so you know exactly what “up to” means for your situation.

How much does home insurance cost in Spain for renters vs. owners?

Renters' contents-only policies are usually cheaper than owners' building-plus-contents policies — your comparison will show real numbers for your specific case.
