What's covered

What Does Civil Liability Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Property damage to others. Accidental damage you cause to someone else's belongings or home.
  • Injury to another person. Accidental bodily injury you cause, whether at home, at a friend's, or in public.
  • Family liability (RC familiar). Extends to accidental damage caused by children, dependents or pets who live with you.
  • Sports & hobby liability. Damage caused while practicing a sport or hobby — sometimes excluded from a home policy's baseline liability.
  • Legal defense costs. Legal representation if you're sued over a covered incident, on many plans.
  • Defense against unfounded claims. Legal defense even when a claim against you turns out to be groundless, not only when you're found liable.
  • Legal bail/bond advance. The insurer can advance a criminal bail bond linked to a covered incident, recovered later.
  • Compensation payments. Court-ordered or negotiated compensation to the injured party, up to the policy limit.
  • Geographic scope. Cover inside Spain as standard — cover abroad is a separate question to confirm.

Worth checking before you buy

The geographic scope — some plans only cover incidents within Spain, which matters if you travel often or split time between countries.

Exact coverage, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and plan you select — full policy conditions are shown before you buy.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Civil Liability Insurance in Spain

What is civil liability insurance, exactly?

It covers you financially and legally when you accidentally cause injury or damage to someone else or their property — it doesn't cover your own losses.

Isn't civil liability already included in my home or car insurance?

Often partially, yes — most home and car policies include a baseline amount. We help you check whether a standalone policy meaningfully adds to that.

Can I get a document in English to show my landlord or school?

Yes — we can provide a plain-language summary alongside your official policy documents.

Does civil liability insurance cover incidents outside Spain?

This varies by insurer and plan — we clarify the geographic scope for the specific plan you choose, before you buy it.
