What's covered

What Does Community / Homeowners' Association Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Common structure. Roof, facade, stairwells and other shared structural elements.
  • Electrical installation damage. Failures in the building's shared wiring, fuse boxes and motors, distinct from damage inside a flat.
  • Elevators. Damage and, on some plans, liability related to elevator use — often needs confirming as included.
  • Water & fire damage to common areas. Damage originating in or affecting shared spaces, as distinct from damage inside an individual flat.
  • Theft in common areas. Mailboxes, intercoms and communal furniture, separate from theft inside an individual flat.
  • Common-area liability. Injury to a resident or visitor in a shared space like a stairwell or garden.
  • 24-hour emergency assistance. A locksmith, plumber, electrician or glazier dispatched to shared spaces, any time.
  • Legal defense & unpaid-fee collection. Legal costs to pursue owners who fall behind on community fees — one of the most valued inclusions in practice.
  • Shared facilities. Pool, garden equipment, communal lighting and other amenities, where present.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Community / Homeowners' Association Insurance in Spain

What is community insurance, exactly?

It's insurance for the shared parts of a residential building — stairwells, roofs, elevators, and common areas — things individual home insurance doesn't cover.

Is community insurance mandatory in Spain?

Requirements can vary by region and building type — we help clarify what actually applies to your specific comunidad.

Who is responsible for arranging community insurance — the association or individual owners?

Typically the homeowners' association (comunidad de propietarios) arranges this on behalf of all owners, funded through community fees.
