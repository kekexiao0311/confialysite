What's covered

What Does Life Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Lump-sum death benefit. A single payout to your named beneficiaries if you die during the policy term.
  • Critical illness add-on. An early payout if you're diagnosed with a covered serious illness, on some plans.
  • Permanent disability cover. A payout tied to Spain's three official disability tiers (IPT, IPA, Gran Invalidez) recognized by Seguridad Social.
  • Premium waiver on disability. Your future premiums are waived if you're declared permanently disabled, so the policy stays active without further payments.
  • Accidental death (double indemnity). Some plans pay out double the sum insured if death results specifically from an accident.
  • Funeral expense cover. A smaller sum paid out quickly toward funeral costs, often included by default alongside the main benefit.
  • Mortgage-linked cover. A decreasing-term policy sized to pay off your remaining mortgage balance if you die.
  • Beneficiary flexibility. Naming and later changing beneficiaries, including ones living outside Spain on most plans.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether the payout amount and term actually match your current life stage — policies bought years ago for an old mortgage or income level often no longer match what your family would actually need today.

Exact coverage, limits and exclusions depend on the insurer and plan you select — full policy conditions are shown before you buy.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Life Insurance in Spain

Is life insurance required for a mortgage in Spain?

Many lenders require or strongly recommend it — we help you find a plan that satisfies your lender without overpaying for their preferred option.

Does age or health affect my life insurance quote?

Yes, both typically affect price and terms — this is part of the quote form so the numbers you see are accurate, not a lowball that changes later.

Can I name beneficiaries who live outside Spain?

Usually yes — we confirm the specific details with the plan you choose.
