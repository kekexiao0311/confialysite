What's covered

What Does Transport Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Loss of goods in transit. Goods that go missing while being transported, whether by your vehicle or a courier.
  • Damage in transit. Goods damaged during loading, transport or unloading.
  • Fire, collision & overturning damage. Damage to goods caused by a traffic accident, vehicle fire or rollover during the journey.
  • Water & weather damage. Rain, flooding or storm damage to goods while they're in transit.
  • Theft during transport. Goods stolen from the vehicle or during a delivery stop.
  • Salvage, storage & trans-shipment costs. Extra costs to rescue, temporarily store or re-route goods after a covered incident.
  • Multi-mode cover. Cover across different transport methods (road, courier, sometimes rail/sea) on some plans.
  • International shipments. Cover for imports and exports, not just domestic routes within Spain.
  • Third-party courier cover. Whether cover extends to goods carried by a courier you hire, not just your own vehicle — confirm this explicitly.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether cover applies only to your own vehicle, or also to third-party couriers you use — this is the single biggest gap businesses discover after a claim, not before.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Transport Insurance in Spain

Is transport insurance the same as insuring my delivery vehicle?

No — vehicle insurance (see our car/motorcycle pages) covers the vehicle itself; transport insurance covers the goods being carried, which is a separate risk.

Does this cover goods shipped by courier, not just my own vehicle?

Depends on the plan — some cover multiple transport methods including third-party couriers; we flag which plans do during comparison.

What happens if goods are damaged but not lost or stolen?

Damage is typically covered alongside loss and theft — full terms are shown before you buy so there's no ambiguity at claim time.
