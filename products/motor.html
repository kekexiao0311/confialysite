What's covered

What Does Motorcycle Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Third-party liability (legal minimum). Injury or damage caused to others — required to legally ride in Spain.
  • Theft & fire. Your bike against theft or fire, typically from mid-tier plans upward.
  • Own-vehicle damage. Repairs to your own bike after an accident, available on comprehensive plans.
  • Rider accident cover. Personal injury cover for the rider, sometimes separate from the vehicle policy.
  • Passenger cover. Injury to a pillion passenger — not automatic on every policy, worth confirming if you ride two-up.
  • Roadside assistance. Towing home or to a garage after a breakdown — especially useful since a broken-down bike can't just be left at the roadside.
  • Legal defense for traffic incidents. Legal representation after an accident or a fine dispute.
  • Vandalism. Scratches or deliberate damage while parked — relevant since bikes are usually left outdoors.
  • Gear cover. Helmet and riding jacket damaged in a covered accident — not included on every plan.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether gear (helmet, jacket) is actually covered if damaged in a covered accident — many basic plans cover only the bike, not what you were wearing.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Motorcycle Insurance in Spain

Is motorcycle insurance mandatory in Spain?

Yes — third-party liability cover is legally required, the same as for cars.

Does engine size affect my motorcycle insurance price?

Significantly, yes — larger engines are typically priced higher; we show you real numbers for your specific bike rather than a generic estimate.

Is a scooter treated the same as a motorcycle for insurance purposes?

Requirements and pricing can differ by engine size and license category — this is accounted for automatically in your quote.
