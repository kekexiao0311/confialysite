What's covered

What Does Health Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Outpatient consultations & specialists. GP visits and specialist appointments, usually without a referral and with shorter waits than the public system.
  • Hospitalization & surgery. Room, theatre and surgeon fees for planned and emergency procedures within the insurer's private network.
  • Diagnostic tests. Blood work, imaging (X-ray, MRI, CT) and lab results, often available faster than through Seguridad Social.
  • Emergency care. Same-day admission to a private A&E, plus ambulance transport when the situation is serious.
  • Oncology treatment. Chemotherapy, radiotherapy and cancer specialists, typically without a lifetime cap on the larger insurers' plans.
  • Second medical opinion. A free international second opinion on serious diagnoses — a standard add-on on most mid-to-premium plans.
  • Mental health support. A set number of psychology or psychiatry sessions per year, now included on more plans than a few years ago.
  • Maternity & paediatric care. Many plans cover maternity and children's specialists after a waiting period that varies by insurer.
  • Dental & optical add-ons. Basic dental cleanings and eye tests are bundled into many plans; major dental work is usually a separate policy.

Worth checking before you buy

Waiting periods for pre-existing conditions, and whether your preferred hospital or doctor network is actually included — ask what's excluded, not just what's covered.

Frequently asked

Frequently Asked Questions About Health Insurance in Spain

How much does health insurance cost in Spain?

It depends heavily on age, location, and whether you want basic or full coverage — our comparison shows you real prices for your specific situation instead of a generic range.

Can I switch health insurance providers at any time?

Usually yes, though timing and waiting periods depend on your current and new policy — we'll walk you through it so you don't lose coverage in the gap.

Will a pre-existing condition affect my quote?

It can affect price or terms depending on the insurer — this is asked upfront so the quotes you see are accurate, not a bait-and-switch price.

Is private health insurance worth it if I already have Seguridad Social?

Many residents use both — private cover for speed and choice of doctor, public healthcare as the safety net. We can help you think through whether it's worth it for your situation.
