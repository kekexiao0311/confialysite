What's covered

What Does Business Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Business property & equipment. Your premises, fixtures, stock and equipment against fire, water damage and theft.
  • General liability. Injury or property damage to a customer or visitor on your premises.
  • Business interruption. Lost income if a covered event forces you to stop trading temporarily.
  • Glass & signage breakage. Shopfront windows and exterior signs — a common claim for any street-facing business.
  • 24/7 emergency assistance. A locksmith, plumber or electrician dispatched urgently after an incident at your premises.
  • Temporary relocation. Rent for alternative premises if yours becomes unusable after a covered claim.
  • Cash & valuables on-site. Theft of cash from a till or safe, usually capped lower than general contents cover.
  • Employee-related liability. Cover if a staff member is injured at work, where relevant to your setup.
  • Goods & stock in transit. Cover for stock while it's on the move, separate from your main property cover — confirm if you need this alongside our Seguro de Transporte line.

Worth checking before you buy

Whether your specific business activity is excluded — some plans exclude higher-risk trades (e.g. food handling, construction) by default, so confirm your exact activity code is covered.

Frequently Asked Questions About Business Insurance in Spain

Do I need business insurance as an autónomo in Spain?

Not always legally required, but often expected by landlords, clients, or contracts — we help you check what actually applies to your situation.

Does business insurance cover my physical shop or studio?

Property cover is typically included or available as an add-on — confirm the specific limit during comparison.

Can I get cover before I officially open my business?

Some insurers allow this — we flag which plans support pre-opening cover so you're protected from day one, not week two.
