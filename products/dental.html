What's covered

What Does Dental Insurance Cover in Spain?

  • Routine checkups & cleanings. Usually one or two annual checkups plus a professional cleaning, often at no extra cost.
  • X-rays & diagnostics. Standard dental X-rays needed to plan treatment.
  • Basic fillings. Cavity treatment on natural teeth, typically covered at 100% or close to it.
  • Emergency dental care. Pain relief and urgent treatment for infections or accidents, usually with a same-week appointment guarantee.
  • Root canals & extractions. Often billed at a fixed co-pay rather than fully free, unlike simple fillings.
  • Gum disease treatment (periodontics). Deep cleaning and periodontal treatment, sometimes capped or excluded on entry-level plans.
  • Paediatric dentistry. Children's checkups and preventive sealants, usually bundled free into family plans.
  • Orthodontics discount. A percentage reduction on braces or aligners — rarely full cover.
  • Major procedures (implants, crowns). Usually a discount rather than full cover — confirm the real percentage before assuming it's included.
